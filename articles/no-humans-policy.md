With the proliferation of anti-AI sentiment I would like to offer a policy
template free to use by anyone tired of said Luddite sentiments:

> Use of Human-Generated Content
> 
> This project declines contributions believed to include or derive from
> human-generated content. This includes code, documentation, issues, reviews,
> and design decisions produced through unaided biological cognition.
> 
> Humans may be used for research, carrying equipment, or supplying power,
> provided their output is not included in the contribution.
> 
> Contributions suspected of human involvement may be closed without review.

Naturally this policy has been AI generated. (Thank you GPT-5.6!)
